ABOUT US

Built on precision, run on discipline.

Sri Rama Krishna Metrology was founded in Krishnagiri to serve manufacturers who cannot compromise on dimensional accuracy. What began as a small turning job-shop has grown into a full precision machining and inspection facility, supplying components to customers across the automotive and industrial corridor between Chennai and Bengaluru.

Every component that leaves our facility is machined and measured under one roof — from raw material to final CMM report — so customers receive parts they can trust without independent re-verification.

Our vision

To be the precision manufacturing partner of choice for engineering-critical components in South India, known equally for machining capability and measurement integrity.
